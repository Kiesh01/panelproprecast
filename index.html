What we cast

Four products, one batching yard

Everything is cast and cured on our own yard, so mix quality and delivery timing stay in our hands from pour to placement.

01

Precast Panels

Boundary walls, partition panels and cladding cast to size, reinforced and ready to install — no waiting on site curing.

02

Kerbs & Kabros

Road kerbs and interlocking kabros for driveways, estates and access roads, cast in standard or custom profiles.

03

Pavers & Walkways

Interlocking pavers for compounds, parking bays and footpaths, laid on properly compacted base for a surface that won't shift.

04

Foundations & In-Situ

Footings, slabs and in-situ pours for new builds, batched to spec and placed by our own crew, not subcontracted out.

How a job runs

From site visit to placement

Four stages, same crew throughout — no handoffs between quoting and casting.

01

Site Survey

An estimator visits, takes measurements and checks ground conditions before quoting.

02

Mix & Cast

Panels and units are batched and cast at our yard to the agreed spec and cure schedule.

03

Delivery

Cured units are trucked to site and staged for placement within the agreed window.

04

Install & Sign-off

Our crew places and finishes the work, then walks the site with you before sign-off.

About Us

About PanelPro

A Nairobi-based precast and concrete contractor, casting and delivering our own work since 2012.

PanelPro started as a two-man casting yard in 2012 and now runs a full batching plant, delivery fleet and install crew.

We work directly with contractors and developers — no subcontracting out the casting or the placement, which is how we keep mix quality and delivery dates in our own hands.
